# Arms License

The Dastak – KP Citizens app is Khyber Pakhtunkhwa's official arms-licensing platform, managed by the Home Department and operated by the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Information Technology Board (KPITB). It handles 5 arms-licensing services end to end: New/Fresh License, Renewal, Duplicate Issuance, Transfer, and Retainer Addition. The app is mobile-only (Android/iOS, no citizen web portal); every service shares the same starting point — CNIC + mobile number registration, OTP verification, and biometric verification at a NADRA Kiosk, e-Sahulat Center, or partner bank, with identity cross-checked against NADRA and Police records. Payments are made in-app via PAYMIR. Standard processing time is up to 45 days (per the Right to Services Act), longer for restricted-bore weapons. Support: Home Department Monitoring \& Evaluation Team, 091-9223594 / 091-9214124.

## New (Fresh) Arms License Application

First-time application by a citizen who does not currently hold an arms license, applying for a Non-Prohibited Bore (NPB) weapon license.

**Eligibility:** Pakistani citizen, valid CNIC, resident of KP; minimum age per Pakistan Arms Rules 2023 (auto-verified via CNIC); no disqualifying criminal record; medically fit to possess a firearm.

**Steps:** Download the app → Sign Up with CNIC + mobile, verify via OTP → complete profile → select Arms License > New/Fresh Application → fill weapon type/bore and reason for license → upload documents → submit affidavit → pay fee via PAYMIR → complete biometric capture at NADRA Kiosk/e-Sahulat/partner bank → application routes digitally to DC/DPO/Home Department for clearance (no citizen visit needed unless clarification is required) → track status in-app → on approval, a physical license card with QR code is printed and made available.

**Documents required:** Attested CNIC (front \& back), recent passport photo, medical fitness certificate, character certificate (from local DSP/SP), affidavit (completed in-app), proof of residence if different from CNIC (conditional).

**Fee:** Rs. 10,300 (Private/General Public, Provincial, Non-Prohibited bore) or Rs. 20,300 (All-Pakistan validity). Restricted bore (.222/.223/.45) is Rs. 40,300, only offered at All-Pakistan validity — not available Provincial-only. Government Officers pay a flat Rs. 300 (Non-Prohibited, either validity); Officers "on Payment" pay the same rates as Private/General Public. All figures include Rs. 300 card charges, an annual fee of Rs. 1,000–4,000, and a default cartridge allowance (25 or 200 rounds depending on bore) with extra cartridges at Rs. 40–100 each. Paid via PAYMIR at submission.

| Applicant Type | Applicability | Bore | Fee | Card Charges | Per-Year Fee |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Private/General Public | Provincial | Non-Prohibited | Rs. 10,300 | Rs. 300 | Rs. 1,000 |
| Private/General Public | All-Pakistan | Non-Prohibited | Rs. 20,300 | Rs. 300 | Rs. 2,000 |
| Private/General Public | All-Pakistan | Restricted (.222/.223/.45) | Rs. 40,300 | Rs. 300 | Rs. 4,000 |

| Govt. Officer on Payment | All-Pakistan | Non-Prohibited | Rs. 20,300 | Rs. 300 | Rs. 2,000 |

**Processing time:** Same-day submission; biometric within a set window; DC/DPO/Home Department clearance up to 45 days (longer for restricted-bore weapons); card printing a few days after approval. 

**Output:** A physical license card with an embedded QR code, plus a digital record verifiable in-app.

**Notes:** Mismatches with CNIC records (spelling, DOB) get flagged for manual correction; the applicant is only contacted if clarification is needed.

## Renewal of Arms License

Renewal of an existing, previously-issued arms license before it lapses or shortly after expiry.

**Eligibility:** Must already hold a valid or recently-expired license linked to their CNIC; no new disqualifying criminal record since issuance; if the weapon was purchased from a dealer, it must still be mapped to that dealer.

**Steps:** Log in → 'My Licenses' → select the eligible license → 'Renew License' → confirm/update details → upload any updated documents (e.g. fresh medical certificate if required) → re-confirm the eligibility affidavit → pay renewal fee via PAYMIR → biometric re-verification if prompted → submit for DC/DPO/Home Department clearance → track status → collect the renewed card once approved.

**Documents required:** Original license number (auto-fetched, no upload), attested CNIC, recent photo, updated medical certificate (conditional, if previous expired), updated character certificate (conditional).

**Fee:** Rs. 5,300 (Private/General Public, Provincial) or Rs. 10,300 (All-Pakistan, Non-Prohibited); restricted bore (.222/.223/.45) is Rs. 20,300 (All-Pakistan only). Government Officers pay a flat Rs. 300 (Non-Prohibited, either validity); Officers "on Payment" pay the same rates as Private/General Public. Late renewal adds Rs. 4/day (Provincial), Rs. 6/day (All-Pakistan Non-Prohibited), or Rs. 22/day (All-Pakistan restricted bore) — Renewal is the only Arms License service with a late fee.

**Processing time:** Same-day submission; biometric re-verification within days if triggered; departmental clearance up to 45 days; card reprint a few days after approval.

**Output:** Renewed license card with updated validity dates, QR code, and refreshed digital record.

**Notes:** Renewing after expiry incurs the late fee above (Rs. 4–22/day depending on category) and may trigger stricter re-verification.

## Duplicate License Issuance

Issuance of a duplicate license card when the original is lost, stolen, or damaged, without changing licensing terms.

**Eligibility:** Must hold an active license record; a police report/FIR is typically required if lost or stolen.

**Steps:** Log in → 'My Licenses' → 'Apply for Duplicate / Copy Conversion' → state the reason (lost, stolen, damaged) → upload loss report/FIR if applicable → confirm details → pay duplicate-issuance fee via PAYMIR → biometric re-verification if requested → submit for departmental review → track status until the duplicate card is approved and printed.

**Documents required:** Attested CNIC, loss report/FIR (conditional), photo of damaged card (conditional), recent passport photo.

**Fee:** Flat Rs. 3,300, paid via PAYMIR — the same fee regardless of applicant type, applicability, or bore restriction.

**Processing time:** Same-day submission; a few days to verify the loss/damage claim; clearance generally faster than a fresh application but can extend up to 45 days; card printing a few days after approval.

**Output:** A reissued duplicate card carrying the same license number, with a fresh QR code.

## Transfer of Arms License

Transfer of a registered weapon/license from the current holder to another eligible person (sale, inheritance, gift), always mediated through a registered arms dealer.

**Eligibility:** Current holder must have an active, valid license for the weapon; receiving party must independently qualify (same checks as a fresh application); the transfer must go through a government-registered arms dealer.

**Steps:** Current holder selects 'Transfer License / Ownership' → enters receiving party's CNIC → selects/confirms the mediating dealer → uploads the sale/transfer agreement → receiving party gets an in-app notification to accept and complete their own verification if not already registered → both parties confirm digitally → transfer fee paid via PAYMIR → receiving party completes biometric verification → application routes to DC/DPO/Home Department for clearance of both parties → track status → on approval, a new license record is issued to the receiving party and the previous holder's record is closed for that weapon.

**Documents required:** Attested CNIC of both parties, existing license details (system record), sale/transfer agreement or affidavit, character certificate of receiving party, medical fitness certificate of receiving party.

**Fee:** Transfer fee via PAYMIR; the dealer may also charge a separate service/documentation fee outside the app.

**Processing time:** Same-day submission (pending both parties responding); dealer confirmation a few days; departmental clearance up to 45 days; new card issuance a few days after approval.

**Output:** New license card in the receiving party's name; original holder's record updated to reflect the transfer.

**Notes:** Direct citizen-to-citizen transfer outside dealer mapping is not permitted — a registered dealer must always mediate.

## Retainer Addition

Adding an eligible blood relative (son, daughter, brother, or sister) as an authorized retainer permitted to use/carry the licensed weapon under the primary holder's license.

**Eligibility:** Requesting party must be the primary, active license holder; retainer must be a blood relative — only son, daughter, brother, or sister are permitted; retainer must independently clear NADRA and Police verification.

**Steps:** Primary holder opens the relevant license under 'My Licenses' → 'Add Retainer' → enters retainer's CNIC and relationship → system validates the relationship against NADRA family-tree records → uploads supporting documents → retainer gets a prompt to confirm and complete their own verification if not already registered → retainer completes biometric verification → primary holder pays the retainer-addition fee via PAYMIR → request routes to DC/DPO/Home Department for clearance → track status until approval.

**Documents required:** Attested CNIC of primary holder and retainer, proof of blood relationship (Family Registration Certificate/B-Form), recent photo of retainer, character certificate of retainer.

**Fee:** Retainer-addition fee, paid via PAYMIR at submission.

**Processing time:** Same-day to a few days for submission and relationship validation; retainer biometric verification is applicant-scheduled; departmental clearance up to 45 days; updated record a few days after approval.

**Output:** Updated license record listing the approved retainer, permitting them to use the weapon under the primary holder's license.

# Driving License

Driving License is a module inside the Dastak app covering 7 service types: Learner Permit, Endorsement, International Driving Permit (IDP), New Permanent License, Renewal, Duplicate, and Public Service Vehicle (PSV) License. Every route shares the same first steps — application submission through the app/counter, then NADRA Verification with a fixed Rs. 140 NADRA fee — before splitting into a fee/payment review and then service-specific stages. There are two broad route "shapes": a shorter 5-stage flow used for Learner-category (Learner Permit and Learner-stage Endorsement) applications that ends in a digital-only download (no physical card), and a longer flow (9 to 11+ stages) used for Permanent-category applications (IDP, New, Renewal, Duplicate, PSV, Permanent-stage Endorsement), which goes through Motor Vehicle Examiner (MVE) practical testing and/or Medical Verification and Motor Licensing Authority (MLA) review before a physical card is printed and collected. At almost every stage, an unpaid or expired payment voucher does not cancel the application — it can be "renewed on request," looping back to the same payment step. "LTV"/"HTV" (vehicle category abbreviations) and "MLA"/"MVE" appear throughout as desk/category names; exact fees vary by vehicle category and license type (see the fee reference table under Renewal, below, for a wider sample).

## Learner Permit

A first-time Learner's License (Learner/LTV) application — the shortest and simplest driving-license route.

**Route (5 stages):** 1) Applicant Submission (Dastak Portal/Counter) → 2) NADRA Verification (NADRA Verification Office, Rs. 140 NADRA fee) → 3) Payment Review (processing payment, typically \~Rs. 1,265) → 4) Final Approval (MLA Office) → 5) Learner Permit Download (Applicant Portal).

**Fee (typical sample):** Application Fee \~Rs. 1,265 + NADRA Fee Rs. 140 = \~Rs. 1,405 total.

**Key rules:** There is no physical card printing or counter collection in this route — the final output is a digital download from the Applicant Portal. If the payment voucher expires unpaid, the application is not cancelled; it can be renewed on request, looping back to the Payment Review step. Each stage is tagged Completed, Current, or Upcoming, which can be used to answer "where is my application" questions.

## Endorsement

Adding a new vehicle category (e.g. LTV, HTV, or both together) to an existing Learner or Permanent license.

**Two variants exist:** an Endorsement against a Learner License follows the same 5-stage Learner-category flow and fee structure as a fresh Learner Permit (\~Rs. 1,265 + Rs. 140 NADRA fee) — requesting two categories at once (LTV+HTV) does not change the flow or fee. An Endorsement against a Permanent License follows the longer 11-stage Permanent-category flow (Applicant Submission → NADRA Verification → Payment Review → Motor Vehicle Examiner → Medical Verification → Motor Licensing Authority → Card Payment Review → Final Approval → Digital License Issued → Card Sent for Printing → Applicant Collects Card), with fees that vary by vehicle category (a sample HTV Endorsement showed an Application Fee of \~Rs. 8,965 + Card Charges Rs. 1,800 + NADRA Fee Rs. 140).

**Key rules:** The route template (Learner-style 5-stage vs. Permanent-style 11-stage) is determined by which license category is being endorsed, not by the fact that it's an endorsement. The Permanent-category route ends with a physical card (digital issuance first, then printing, then in-person collection); the Learner-category route ends with a digital-only download.

## International Driving Permit (IDP)

Application for an International Driving Permit, linked to an existing LTV license — the longest of the standard routes at 9 stages, with two separate payment checkpoints.

**Route (9 stages):** 1) Applicant Submission → 2) NADRA Verification (Rs. 140 NADRA fee) → 3) Payment Review (\~Rs. 11,000 processing payment) → 4) International Approval → 5) Card Payment Review (\~Rs. 1,650 card payment) → 6) Final Approval → 7) Digital License Issued → 8) Card Sent for Printing → 9) Applicant Collects Card.

**Fee (typical sample):** Application Fee \~Rs. 11,000 + Card Charges \~Rs. 1,650 + NADRA Fee Rs. 140 ≈ Rs. 12,790 total.

**Key rules:** There are two independent voucher-expiry loops — one at the Payment Review stage (Stage 3) and one at the Card Payment Review stage (Stage 5); an expired voucher at one does not affect the other, and each can be "renewed on request" without restarting the whole application. Unlike the Learner route, the IDP ends with a physical card: digital issuance first, then printing, then in-person collection.

## New Permanent License

A first-time application for a full Permanent driving license (not a Learner Permit).

**Route (11 stages, typical):** Applicant Submission → NADRA Verification (Rs. 140) → Payment Review (\~Rs. 6,150) → Motor Vehicle Examiner → Medical Verification → Motor Licensing Authority → Card Payment Review (\~Rs. 1,800) → Final Approval → Digital License Issued → Card Sent for Printing → Applicant Collects Card.

**Fee (typical sample):** Application Fee \~Rs. 6,150–7,950 (varies by vehicle class) + Card Charges Rs. 1,800 + NADRA Fee Rs. 140.

**Key rules:** Same conditional loops as other Permanent-category routes — application-voucher expiry, MVE test failure (retried at the next available district slot), medical unfitness (reschedule and retry), MLA data deficiency (return to applicant for correction, then recheck), and card-voucher expiry. Digital license issuance happens before the physical card is printed and collected.

## Renewal of Permanent License

Renewing an existing Permanent driving license before or shortly after it lapses.

**Route:** Same overall Permanent-category shape as New/Duplicate/Endorsement (Applicant Submission → NADRA Verification → Payment Review → MVE → Medical Verification → MLA → Card Payment Review → Final Approval → Digital Issuance → Printing → Collection), but with an added **Late Renewal Surcharge** fee line specific to this route.

**Fee (typical sample):** Application Fee Rs. 2,365 + Late Renewal Surcharge Rs. 3,500 + Card Charges Rs. 1,800 + NADRA Fee Rs. 140 = Portal Payable Rs. 7,665 (Rs. 7,805 with NADRA fee included).

**Broader fee reference (varies by vehicle class and how late the renewal is):**

| License Type | Vehicle Class   | Normal     | After 30 Days | After 3 Years |
| ------------ | --------------- | ---------- | ------------- | ------------- |
| Permanent    | LTV + HTV + PSV | Rs. 10,765 | Rs. 11,265    | Rs. 12,765    |
| Permanent    | Tractor         | Rs. 4,165  | Rs. 4,665     | Rs. 7,665     |
| Permanent    | Hill Road       | Rs. 5,815  | Rs. 6,315     | Rs. 7,815     |
| Permanent    | LTV PSV         | Rs. 4,165  | Rs. 4,665     | Rs. 7,665     |

**Key rules:** Renewal fees increase the longer the applicant waits past expiry (normal → after 30 days → after 3 years), on top of the standard Late Renewal Surcharge. Before submission, the application sits in "Draft" with the applicant themselves as the active party — this is distinct from "submitted and pending NADRA verification."

## Duplicate License

Reissuing a duplicate of an already-held Permanent driving license — can be requested standalone, or combined with a Renewal in one application ("Duplicate + Renewal").

**Route (standalone Duplicate, 9 stages):** Similar shape to the IDP route but with a Motor Licensing Authority (MLA) review step in place of "International Approval." The MLA step is the one stage with a genuine content-correction loop: a deficiency in the applicant's data (name, father's name, address, date of birth, CNIC, or attachments) sends the case back to the applicant for correction, then back to MLA for recheck, as many times as needed.

**Route (combined Duplicate + Renewal, 11 stages):** The Duplicate route's payment/MLA/card structure plus two extra inspection stages — an MVE practical test and a Medical fitness check. Five independent conditional loops exist on this combined route: application-voucher expiry, MVE failure, medical unfitness, MLA data deficiency, and card-voucher expiry — each returns only its own stage, not the whole application.

**Fee (typical samples):** Standalone Duplicate: Application Fee \~Rs. 2,365 + Card Charges Rs. 1,800 + NADRA Fee Rs. 140 ≈ Rs. 4,305 total. Combined Duplicate + Renewal: Application Fee \~Rs. 4,565 + Card Charges Rs. 1,800 + NADRA Fee Rs. 140 ≈ Rs. 6,505 total (higher due to the extra Renewal component).

**Key rules:** As with other Permanent-category routes, the digital license is issued before the physical card is printed and collected in person.

## Public Service Vehicle (PSV) License

A Public Service Vehicle license application, tied to a Permanent License and an HTV PSV vehicle category — the route used for commercial/passenger-carrying vehicle drivers.

**Route (11 stages):** Applicant Submission → NADRA Verification (Rs. 140) → Payment Review (\~Rs. 8,150) → Medical Verification → Motor Vehicle Examiner → Motor Licensing Authority → Card Payment Review (\~Rs. 1,800) → Final Approval → Digital License Issued → Card Sent for Printing → Applicant Collects Card.

**Fee (typical sample):** Application Fee \~Rs. 8,150 + Card Charges Rs. 1,800 + NADRA Fee Rs. 140 ≈ Rs. 10,090 total.

**Key rules:** The PSV route places Medical Verification before the Motor Vehicle Examiner test — the reverse order of most other Permanent-category routes (which do MVE before Medical). The same conditional loops apply (voucher expiry, medical unfitness → reschedule, MVE failure → next available slot, MLA deficiency → correction).

## Authorized Medical Hospitals for Driving License

Medical fitness verification for Permanent-category driving license applications (New, Renewal, Duplicate, Endorsement, PSV) must be completed at an authorized government hospital. Authorized facilities include District Headquarters (DHQ) Hospitals across KP districts (e.g. Peshawar, Mardan, Kohat, Bannu, Swat, Dera Ismail Khan, Chitral Upper/Lower, Dir Upper/Lower, Malakand, Shangla, Buner, Torghar, Kohistan Upper/Lower, Karak, Hangu, Kurram, Orakzai, North/South Waziristan), plus specific facilities in Peshawar such as the Police Service Hospital and Naserullah Babar Hospital. A citizen should be directed to their nearest DHQ Hospital or one of these named facilities for the Medical Verification stage.

# MVRS (Motor Vehicle Registration System)

MVRS handles vehicle registration and related services for Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. It covers 4 citizen-facing services: New Vehicle Registration, e-Transfer of Ownership, Token Tax Payment, and Additional Services (e.g. Duplicate Smart Card). A Payment Slip ID (PSID) is generated for fee payment across all four services, and NADRA biometric verification is used for identity-sensitive steps like ownership transfer.

## New Vehicle Registration

Registering a new or imported vehicle.

**Steps:** Citizen initiates request → selects registration type and vehicle category → enters owner and vehicle details → uploads required documents (e.g. invoice, CNIC) → system calculates the fee → generates a Payment Slip ID (PSID) → citizen pays via mobile app/bank → payment is verified automatically → a physical vehicle inspection is scheduled/performed → **if inspection is approved:** a registration number is assigned, the smart card is sent to printing, and it's dispatched by courier (application complete) → **if inspection is not approved:** the application is rejected with the deficiency marked (no automatic retry shown).

## e-Transfer of Ownership

A fully digital transfer of a vehicle between a seller and buyer, without both needing to visit an office together.

**Steps:** Seller initiates the e-transfer → enters the buyer's CNIC and details → system notifies the buyer → buyer opens the app and reviews the request → **if the buyer declines:** the transfer is cancelled (no retry) → **if the buyer accepts:** the system calculates the transfer fee, generates a PSID for the buyer, the buyer pays, and completes NADRA biometric verification → **if verification fails:** the buyer retries biometric verification (loops back, does not cancel the transfer) → **if verification succeeds:** the ownership record is updated, a new smart card is printed, and it's dispatched to the new owner.

## Token Tax Payment

The automated annual vehicle tax payment process.

**Steps:** Citizen enters the vehicle registration number → system queries the vehicle's ledger → checks for any arrears/fines (if found, they're added to the current year's tax; if not, only the current year's tax is calculated) → rebates or penalties are applied → a consolidated PSID is generated → citizen pays via mobile app/bank → payment gateway confirms receipt → the vehicle's tax expiry date is updated → a digital payment receipt/certificate is generated.

**Fee reference (sample 1LINK PSID payment, Peshawar district):** Token Tax Rs. 4,000 + Income Tax Rs. 2,500 − Rebate Rs. 800 = Total Rs. 5,700 (actual amount varies by vehicle and district).

**Key rules:** This is described as fully automated with no rejection or failure branch — every path leads to a completed payment.

## Duplicate Smart Card

Requesting a replacement physical identifier (e.g. a lost, stolen, or damaged smart card) — the general pattern used for MVRS "Additional Services."

**Steps:** Citizen selects Additional Service → chooses 'Duplicate Smart Card' → states the reason (if lost/stolen: uploads a copy of the police report/FIR; if damaged: no FIR required) → system calculates a fixed fee → a PSID is generated → citizen pays the fee → an Excise Officer verifies the request → **if not approved:** the request is rejected with a reason given → **if approved:** it goes to the printing queue and is dispatched by courier to the citizen.

# Asan Karobar (Business Licensing)

Asan Karobar is the Dastak app's business-licensing platform, covering business registrations and licenses across several KP departments/authorities through one unified citizen journey. The general in-app path is the same regardless of which specific license or department is involved: open the app → select "Get Registration" → fill profile → choose the specific service (see below) → fill and submit the application → pay → complete NADRA Biometric (e-Sahulat) verification → a designated officer reviews the application → license approved → license appears on the citizen's profile. Every one of the specific services below replaces what used to be a fully manual, in-person, paper-based process (physical visits, bank-draft payments, mailed documents, no tracking) with this fully digital one (online forms, 1Link/Paymir digital payment, digital document upload, real-time tracking, and a digitally-issued certificate/license, usually with a QR code).

## Firm Registration

Registering a firm with the KP Government Firms Registration authority.

**What changed from the old manual process:** documents are now submitted online instead of manually; partner identity is verified through NADRA e-Sahulat instead of a physical visit to the AD Office; payment is digital instead of through Treasury/National Bank; the output is an online e-certificate with QR-code verification instead of a manually issued Form H certificate; real-time application tracking is now available (there was none before); and internal approvals (AD → Deputy Director → Director → DG) are now automated instead of manual.

**Result:** office visits reduced from 3 to none; payment and partner verification both simplified to a fully online process.

## SIDB Plot Allotment

Plot allotment through the Sarhad Industrial Development Board (SIDB).

**What changed from the old manual process:** application submission moved from paper forms and newspaper ads to fully online submission via the app/portal; fee payment moved from physical bank drafts to instant digital payment; document submission moved from physical mailing (with risk of loss) to secure digital upload; deficiency handling moved from difficult, rejection-prone corrections to easy in-app correction; the review workflow (Estate Incharge → Joint Director → Deputy MD → MD) moved from manual, office-to-office file movement to a digital, automated approval workflow; and the Letter of Acceptance (LOA) moved from manual printing and mailing to an auto-generated digital document.

**Result:** required visits dropped from a minimum of 4 to none; real-time tracking is now available where there was previously no tracking.

## KPEZDMC Plot Allotment

Plot allotment through the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Economic Zones Development \& Management Company (KPEZDMC).

**What changed from the old manual process:** applicants previously needed a physical visit and relied on department-specific website info — now they apply fully online with no visit required; fees moved from bank drafts to digital 1Link/Paymir payment; documents moved from manual attachment and physical filing to digital upload; deficiency handling moved from manual, cumbersome communication to easy in-app handling; review (across BP\&S and HoD levels) moved from manual movement to a streamlined digital workflow; and the Plot Allotment Letter (PAL) moved from email/portal delivery after manual processing to direct in-app availability.

## Culture \& Tourism Authority (CTA) Registration

Registration for Travel Agency, Hotel, Restaurant, and Guest House businesses through the Culture \& Tourism Authority.

**What changed from the old manual process:** applicants previously visited a website and filled forms manually — now they apply fully online through the app; fees moved from physical bank payment to digital 1Link/Paymir; documents moved from manual attachment to digital upload and instant online submission; deficiency handling moved from slow manual correction to easy in-app correction; scrutiny, review/inspection (AD/DD stages), and approval workflows all moved from manual, physical-file-based coordination to a routed digital process; and license issuance moved from manual print/portal sharing to an auto-generated license available on the app as a PDF download.

## Halal Food Authority (HFA) Food Business NOC

A food-business No Objection Certificate (NOC) through the Halal Food Authority (also referred to as "KPFS" in some app screens — same service).

**What changed from the old manual process:** application entry used to depend on separate, fragmented channels (field team or district office) — now the business owner applies online through the app with all entry points integrated into one centralized process; the payment challan moved from a department-generated process to an auto-generated digital challan; payment moved from a separate, less convenient process to digital payment through Easypaisa, JazzCash, MCB App, UBL App, or 1Link/Paymir (this is the only Asan Karobar service that names specific wallet/bank-app options); and the license/NOC moved from internal-department issuance and separate collection to automatic issuance available directly on the Dastak app.

## Other Registrations \& Licenses (FoodGrain, Mills, Godown)

The generic Asan Karobar workflow used for services not covered by the five named services above — explicitly covering Food Grain License, Mills License, and Godown Registration. (See the separate "FoodGrain Licensing" section below for the detailed FoodGrain-specific process.)

**What changed from the old manual process:** application submission moved from manual paper forms at the office to online submission through the app; documents moved from physical attachment to digital upload; fees moved from physical bank/treasury payment to online digital payment; scrutiny and review moved from manual file handling to a digital online workflow; approvals moved from manual office-to-office routing to a digital workflow; tracking moved from unavailable to live, real-time tracking; and certificate/license issuance moved from manual collection to auto-generated online availability.

# FoodGrain Licensing

The FoodGrain Licensing System issues 6 types of licenses for grain shops and mills. New shop and mill licenses can be applied for and issued fully online. **Renewals cannot currently be done online — this is an important limitation to tell citizens about (see "Renewals" below).**

## Licence Types

| Type    | What it's for                          | Available now?          |
| ------- | -------------------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Type 1  | New wholesaler shop licence            | Yes                     |
| Type 2  | New retailer shop licence              | Yes                     |
| Type 3  | New retailer + wholesaler shop licence | Yes                     |
| Type 11 | New mill licence                       | Yes                     |
| Type 4  | Individual licence renewal             | No — not yet available |
| Type 12 | Mill licence renewal                   | No — not yet available |

## How to Apply — Shop Licence (Types 1, 2, or 3)

Applying for a wholesaler, retailer, or combined retailer+wholesaler licence follows these steps: 1) Identity — applicant provides identity details (starts as a draft) → 2) Addresses — applicant provides address details → 3) Business + Goods — applicant provides business and goods details → 4) Documents + Submit — applicant uploads documents and submits → 5) NADRA Biometric — applicant completes biometric verification → 6) DFC Scrutiny — a District Food Controller (DFC) officer reviews the application → 7) Initial DFC Decision — the officer approves it, sends it back for correction, or rejects it (see "Possible Outcomes" below) → 8) AFC / Food Inspector — an Assistant Food Controller or Food Inspector adds a recommendation/inspection finding → 9) DFC Final Review → 10) Final DFC Decision — final approval or rejection (see "Possible Outcomes") → 11) Final Approval — moves forward automatically → 12) Voucher + PSID generated → 13) Payment Confirmed → 14) Approved Queue → 15) Licence Issued → 16) PDF + QR — the citizen can access their licence as a PDF with a QR code.

## How to Apply — Mill Licence (Type 11)

Follows the same 16 steps as the shop licence process above, with one difference: at Step 3, the applicant provides additional mill-specific data. Mill applications also go through one extra clearance stage: after the officer review stage (Steps 7–10), a mill application additionally goes to Department Opinions for a mill-only clearance, then to a Licensing Committee, before final approval can proceed. Everything else (payment, issuance, PDF + QR licence) works the same way as for shop licences.

## Possible Outcomes

| Outcome                     | What happens                                                                                                                                    |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Approved (initial or final) | The application moves forward to the next step.                                                                                                 |
| Sent back for correction    | Can happen after the initial review (Step 7). The applicant is asked to correct and resubmit — not rejected.                                   |
| Rejected — initial         | Can happen after the initial review (Step 7). The application process ends.                                                                     |
| Rejected — final           | Can happen after the final review (Step 10). The application process ends, with no correction step at this stage — a final rejection is final. |

## Renewals

Licence renewal (Type 4 for individuals, Type 12 for mills) is **not currently available to citizens.** There is no way to start a renewal application, no review or payment process for one, and no way to issue a renewed licence. If a citizen asks about renewing a FoodGrain licence, tell them this service is not yet available — do not describe renewal steps for it.
